\section{Memory Layout}

We store the 14x7 grid row-major as a flat array of words. Each cell holds an integer code (0 for empty, 1–4 for colours) instead of a full RGB value. This saves memory and makes match logic faster by allowing direct integer comparisons, with a helper function translating codes to colours only at draw time.

\subsection{Data section}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|p{0.45\textwidth}|}
\hline
Label & Size & Contents \\
\hline
\texttt{ADDR\_DSPL}, \texttt{ADDR\_KBRD} & 1 word each & Display and keyboard addresses \\
\texttt{COLOUR\_*} & 1 word each & RGB values for background, wall, player and the four bubble colours \\
\texttt{player\_col} & 1 word & Player's column, 0--6 (starts at 3) \\
\texttt{held\_count} & 1 word & Number of bubbles held \\
\texttt{held\_colour} & 1 word & Colour code being held (0 = none) \\
\texttt{grid} & 98 words (392 bytes) & 14 rows $\times$ 7 columns, row-major; 0 = empty, 1--4 = colour \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Cell (row $r$, column $c$) of \texttt{grid} is at byte offset $(7r + c) \times 4$, with row 0 at the top.

\subsection{Screen layout}

Each unit on the 32 $\times$ 32 display is one word, so unit (row $r$, column $c$) is at address \texttt{0x10008000} $+ 128r + 4c$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
Element & Unit rows & Unit columns \\
\hline
Walls & 1--17 & 1 and 9 (plus all of rows 1 and 17) \\
Bubble grid & 2--15 & 2--8 \\
Player & 16 & \texttt{player\_col} + 2 \\
Held bubbles & 18 and below & \texttt{player\_col} + 2 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
